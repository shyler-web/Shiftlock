Invariant / winning condition
Odd n: only even permutations are reachable.

A row or column shift on an odd board is an even cycle. Legal moves cannot change permutation parity. On a 4×4 board this parity test does not block a state, because one legal shift already changes parity.

Why the glitch can be proved
One shift of n tiles uses n − 1 pair swaps.

For 3×3 and 5×5 boards, every legal row or column rotation is even. A sequence of legal moves stays even, while swapping exactly two tiles is odd. No search is needed: parity is a certificate of impossibility.
